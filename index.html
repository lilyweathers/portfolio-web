01 / ABOUT ME

Curiosity connects
everything I do.

I’m a developer working across the web, AI, and games—with a creative background that connects them.

I build fullstack applications with React, Next.js, and TypeScript, supported by experience with Node.js, databases, and a foundation in Java and Spring. During my frontend internship at Just Value AB, I contributed to commercial websites, improving responsive design, SEO, and usability.

In StayFinder, my accommodation booking project, I developed property search, availability filtering, and booking management for guests and hosts, backed by a Node.js/Hono API, Supabase authentication, and PostgreSQL. The work includes request validation, ownership checks, row-level security, and automated API tests.

As an AI & Game Developer intern at Ratter Studios, I’m working on Stockholm1646, a historical game. My work brings together Unreal Engine, C++, Python, and AI dialogue using local models, including work with AI training. Alongside this, I develop RAG pipelines that turn historical source material into searchable knowledge through document processing and embeddings, using Qdrant as the vector database for storing embeddings and retrieving relevant source material through similarity search.

My background in media production, administration, and customer-facing work shapes how I approach development: with an eye for storytelling, clear communication, and attention to the people behind the experience. I’m continuing to grow across fullstack, AI, and game development, drawn to projects where technical problem-solving and creativity meet.
